What's New & App Guide

🏛️

Safer Borrowing Limits

Maximum loan is automatically capped at 90% of certificate value or redemption value, whichever is less.

🏷️

Clear Certificate Cards

In Self-Sufficient mode, every certificate is now a neat card with clear names directly above every box.

⚡

Auto-Calculated Loan Rate

Loan interest automatically sets itself 2% above your highest certificate rate, matching bank policies.

📅

First Payment Date

Clearly displays your first installment date (5th of 2nd month) for easy budgeting.

🔄

Instant Certificate Sync

When calculating a self-paying loan, your certificates automatically transfer to your loan details with zero re-typing.

🧮

Flexible Loan Calculator

Calculate your monthly installment, loan amount, interest rate, or duration with reducing balance calculations.

🛡️

Secured Loans (CD-Backed)

Check your borrowing limit and see whether your monthly certificate interest covers your loan payment.

⚡

Self-Sufficient Loan Mode

Use your existing certificate to fund a new high-rate certificate that pays off all installments with monthly surplus.

⏩

Early Settlement Calculator

Find out the exact remaining principal, payoff fees, and interest accrued for any settlement date.

📊

Schedule & PDF Reports

View month-by-month repayment breakdown, quarterly stamp tax, and export official PDF or Excel reports.

🔒

100% Private & Offline

Runs completely offline on your device. Your financial numbers are never sent anywhere.
